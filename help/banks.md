# Bank Accounts Help

## Overview

Manage your personal bank accounts linked to banks in the Bank Master.

## Features

-   **Add** a bank account. Select the bank, then enter the account number and account type.
-   **Edit** existing accounts.
-   **Delete** accounts (you are asked to confirm).
-   Account types: `Savings` (default), `Current`, `NRE`, `NRO`
-   Your accounts show the bank, branch, city, account number and type.

## Prerequisites

-   The sysadmin must have added the bank to **Bank Master** first before you can link an account.

## Tips

-   Each account is linked to a bank from the master list.
-   Only your own accounts are visible when logged in as a regular user.
-   Use **Statements** to import bank statements for an account, and **Investments** to record investments against it.
