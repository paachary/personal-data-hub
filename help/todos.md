# Todos & Reminders Help

## Overview

Track your personal tasks and reminders.

> 🔐 Regular users only. The sysadmin sees a restricted message on this page.

## Features

-   **Add** a new todo with a title, due date and priority (🔴 high, 🟡 medium, 🟢 low)
-   **Edit** a todo
-   **Mark** todos as done, or back to pending
-   **Delete** todos (you are asked to confirm)
-   **Filter** the list by **All**, **Pending** or **Done**. Each tab shows a count.
-   Reminders from vault entries with an expiry date also appear here.

## Status Indicators

-   ⚠️ **Overdue**: the due date has passed and the todo is still pending.
-   🔔 **Due soon**: the due date is close.

## Tips

-   Completed todos are hidden from the dashboard's **Pending Todos** count.
-   Desktop notifications are shown for reminders that are due.
-   Sysadmin cannot access your todos.
