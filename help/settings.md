# Settings Help

## Overview

Manage your profile details and change your password.

## Profile

Update your personal and address details, then click **Save Profile**.

-   **Personal info**: first name, last name, email and mobile number
-   **Address**: street, country, state and city. Choose the country first; the state and city lists fill in from an online lookup, so you need internet access to change them.

## Password

Change your login password. You must enter:

-   **Current password**
-   **New password**, which must meet the password policy below

Passwords must be 8 to 50 characters and include at least one:

-   Lowercase letter
-   Uppercase letter
-   Number
-   Special character

## Tips

-   Keep your email and mobile number up to date.
-   The sysadmin can reset your password. If you are locked out, ask the sysadmin.
-   Sysadmin accounts also see **Admin** settings in the sidebar: Bank Master, Instrument Types, Investment Types and User Management.
